Problem Statement & Research Motivation

Bridging the disconnect between latent campus skills and immediate peer necessities

01

Unstructured Communication Channels

Students post emergency book requests, project tutoring calls, and equipment rentals in sprawling 1,024-member WhatsApp or Telegram batches where messages are buried within seconds with zero searchability.

02

Discovery & Pricing Asymmetry

48.0% of students cannot discover qualified peers, and 35.6% cannot set fair micro-gig pricing. Talented coders, designers, and tutors remain idle while peers pay exorbitant third-party rates.

03

Dorm Asset Idleness & Waste

Lab kits, drafters, calculators, textbooks, and monitors sit abandoned at semester ends. NeedHub unlocks circular student commerce, reducing carbon footprint and college living expenses.

The NeedHub Solution Architecture

A structured, verified university marketplace with AI-driven skill matching, peer trust-indexed escrow payments, automated need triage, and dynamic gig pricing.

Data-Driven Product Architecture

NeedHub Application Inferences & Strategic Roadmap

Translating empirical survey distributions and machine learning parameters directly into high-impact app features, retention loops, and sustainable monetization mechanisms.

Dynamic Smart-Pricing Engine

Data Inference: 35.6% of students hesitate to offer gigs because they don't know how much to charge.

Feature Implementation:

NeedHub suggests dynamic micro-pricing (e.g. ₹150 for 1-hr assignment help, ₹500 for UI redesign) based on campus supply-demand indices.

Peer Trust Escrow & Edu Verification

Data Inference: 66.7% prefer peer commerce, but need protection against non-delivery or incomplete work.

Feature Implementation:

College email (@pccoepune.org) single sign-on + UPI escrow holding funds until buyer approves task delivery.

Semester Re-Commerce & Rental Hub

Data Inference: 61.6% report unused dorm items and 66.7% suffer from lost WhatsApp rental inquiries.

Feature Implementation:

Temporary peer rental tracking with deposit automation for lab coats, engineering drafters, and Arduino kits.
