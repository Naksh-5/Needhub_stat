Problem Statement & Empirical Motivation

Quantifying the gap between campus supply and peer necessity

01

WhatsApp & Chat Channel Drowning

Students post exam notes queries, project partner requests, and drafter rentals in 1,000-member groups where messages vanish within minutes. Zero searchability or fulfillment tracking.

02

Discovery & Pricing Hesitation

48.0% cannot discover qualified peers, and 35.6% don't know how to price their services. Talented student coders and tutors remain idle while peers pay high commercial rates.

03

Dorm Asset Idleness & Waste

Drafters, lab coats, Arduino kits, and textbooks are abandoned at semester end. NeedHub unlocks circular micro-rentals and re-commerce, cutting student living costs.

The NeedHub Machine Learning Solution

Using supervised classification to identify campus supply, continuous regression to forecast urgent campus demand spikes, and $k$-Means clustering to target student user archetypes.

Empirical Product Engineering

NeedHub Application Inferences & Strategic Roadmap

Directly translating survey distributions and ML model coefficients into core product features, campus retention loops, and monetization mechanics.

Dynamic Smart-Pricing Engine

Data Finding: 35.6% of students hesitate to offer gigs because they don't know what to charge.

Feature Implementation:

Automated pricing guidance (e.g. ₹150 for 1-hr tutoring, ₹500 for UI fixes) tuned to real-time campus demand.

Peer Trust Escrow & Edu Verification

Data Finding: 66.7% prefer peer solutions, but need delivery protection and collegiate verification.

Feature Implementation:

College email SSO (@pccoepune.org) + instant UPI micro-escrow locking payment until buyer confirms task completion.

Semester Recommerce & Gear Rentals

Data Finding: 61.6% have unused dorm items and 66.7% report lost WhatsApp rental inquiries.

Feature Implementation:

Automated security deposit handling for drafters, lab coats, and engineering calculators with return receipts.
